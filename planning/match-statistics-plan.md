# Football Central — Match Statistics Plan

## Purpose
Give fans detailed statistics for football matches.

## Team Statistics
- Possession percentage
- Total shots
- Shots on target
- Shots off target
- Corners
- Fouls
- Yellow cards
- Red cards
- Offsides
- Passes completed
- Pass accuracy

## Player Statistics
- Goals
- Assists
- Shots
- Shots on target
- Key passes
- Tackles
- Interceptions
- Saves
- Minutes played

## Match Information
- Final score
- Competition
- Stadium
- Referee
- Match date
- Match duration

## Visual Features
- Team comparison charts
- Player performance ratings
- Match statistics dashboard
- Match timeline

## Future Features
- Compare two teams
- Compare individual players
- Season statistics
- Downloadable match reports
