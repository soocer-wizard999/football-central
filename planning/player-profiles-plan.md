# Football Central — Player Profiles Plan

## Purpose
Give fans detailed information about football players from around the world.

## Player Information
- Full name
- Player photo
- Date of birth
- Nationality
- Height
- Position
- Shirt number
- Current club
- Preferred foot

## Player Statistics
- Appearances
- Goals
- Assists
- Minutes played
- Yellow cards
- Red cards
- Clean sheets (for goalkeepers)

## Career History
- Current club
- Previous clubs
- Transfer history
- National team
- International appearances
- Career trophies

## Performance Information
- Goals per season
- Assists per season
- Match ratings
- Recent performances
- Career statistics

## Future Features
- Favorite players
- Player comparisons
- Season-by-season statistics
- Career timeline
- Player search and filters
